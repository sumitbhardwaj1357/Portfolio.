Market Research Future Market Insights

Synthesizing global data into 300+ authoritative industry reports.

Role

Market Research Writer

Output

300+ Long-form Reports
CAGR Forecasting
Competitive Landscapes

The Challenge

B2B audiences don't want fluff; they want actionable data. The challenge was taking massive, complex datasets involving market sizing, regulatory intelligence, and global segmentation, and translating them into readable, authoritative narratives that executives could actually use.

The Approach

I structured a highly systematic writing process. Each report followed a strict hierarchy: Executive Summary, Market Dynamics, Regional Benchmarking, and Competitive Intelligence. By standardizing the format, I was able to rapidly scale production without sacrificing factual accuracy.

The Impact

Delivered over 300 comprehensive reports covering diverse sectors including food technology, nutraceuticals, and healthcare inputs. These reports directly supported the firm's SEO dominance.

Reddit Strategy Community Growth

Building a 2M+ view Reddit engine from zero.

Role

Reddit Strategist & Copywriter

Metrics

2,000,000+

Views in ~3 months

The Truth About Reddit

Reddit hates marketers. If you try to sell, you get banned. The challenge was to build brand authority and massive visibility on the platform without triggering the community's aggressive anti-promotion filters.

The Execution

Instead of posting links, I posted value. I reverse-engineered the subreddits, analyzed what type of long-form stories and data breakdowns the community upvoted, and wrote native content that solved real problems. The brand was positioned as a helpful peer, not a corporation.
